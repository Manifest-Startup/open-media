## What you may do

- **Say what your project is built on:** "based on Open Media", "a fork of Open Media", "built with Open Media's SDK".
- **Say what it works with:** "compatible with Open Media", "works with Open Media", "for Open Media".
- **Federate and interoperate:** connect to Open Media, read or post through its API, MCP servers or WebMCP tools, exchange messages with Open Media users, and say so. Interoperability is the point of the project.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** of Open Media under its name, with the logos as shipped.
- **Write about it:** articles, papers, reviews, tutorials, talks and screenshots may use the name and logo to refer to Open Media.

## What needs a different name

- **Forks and hosted versions** that change the code, or that you run as a service for others, must use a name and logo of their own that are not confusingly similar to Open Media (for example, not "Open Media Pro" or "OpenMedia+"). Keep the attribution line that the [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires: `Based on Open Media (https://github.com/Manifest-Startup/open-media) by Mihir Kavishwar.`
- **App store listings, domain names and social accounts** for your version must not use "Open Media" as their name.

## Always

- Do not imply that the Open Media project or Mihir Kavishwar endorses, sponsors or runs your product or service unless that is true and agreed in writing.
- Do not change the logos or combine them with your own marks.
